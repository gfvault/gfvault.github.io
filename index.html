Our story

Griffin Fletcher started with numbers. A finance degree from Mississippi State taught him to measure value and risk. Then a watch caught his attention, and he found something numbers alone couldn't explain.

What pulled him in was the craft behind the dial: tiny mechanical parts working together without a battery, finishing done by hand and generations of watchmakers chasing accuracy. He bought a watch, sold it and discovered how much he enjoyed finding the right piece at the right price. So he kept going. That passion became GF Vault, a watch company in Collierville, Tennessee, where fair pricing comes first.
