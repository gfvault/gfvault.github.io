About GF Vault

A Tennessee watch company with worldwide reach.

Our story

GF Vault was founded by Griffin Fletcher, a finance guy turned watch obsessive. Trained in finance at Mississippi State University, Griffin was rooted in numbers, value and risk. Then a watch got in the way.

It started as a purchase and became a fascination: the engineering inside the case, the craft on the dial, and centuries of watchmakers chasing accuracy. He bought one, sold it, loved the thrill of finding the right piece at the right price, and has never been able to stop. That hobby grew into GF Vault, a watch company in Collierville, Tennessee, where fair prices and honest condition come first.
