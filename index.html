About GF Vault

GF Vault is a Tennessee-based luxury watch company that buys, sells, trades and sources fine timepieces from Rolex, Tudor, Omega, Cartier, Grand Seiko, IWC and other leading houses.

Every watch is checked for authenticity before it is listed. We describe condition honestly, photograph what you are buying, and ship fully insured.

  • Fair, fast deals. Straightforward pricing and quick payment when we buy.
  • Worldwide shipping. Fully insured, tracked delivery.
